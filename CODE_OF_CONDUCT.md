# Code of Conduct

## Our standard
Be respectful, constructive, and patient.

Examples of expected behavior:
- assuming good intent
- giving specific, technical feedback
- keeping discussion focused on the work
- being welcoming to different levels of experience

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- harassment or personal attacks
- hostile, insulting, or demeaning language
- publishing someone else's private information without permission
- repeated off-topic or disruptive behavior

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, or pull requests that do not match this standard.

## Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the repository owner through GitHub at [@ThalesMMS](https://github.com/ThalesMMS) or see [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) for escalation steps.

Maintainers should acknowledge conduct reports within 7 days. If you do not receive an acknowledgement by then, open a minimal public issue using the support routing and request maintainer follow-up, without including sensitive details.

Reports will be handled confidentially to the extent possible; do not include sensitive details in public issues unless a maintainer asks you to move the discussion there.
